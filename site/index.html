When you can't pick up, the job still gets booked.

What you get

  • Every call, with a summary, in your email and on a page you can open on your phone.
  • Booked jobs with the address, phone number and what the caller said.
  • A short report each Monday: calls answered, after-hours calls, jobs booked.
  • You switch it on and off yourself with your phone's call forwarding. Ten seconds.

What it won't do

  • It won't quote a price for your specific job, or give advice on gas, electrical work or opening equipment.
  • It can make mistakes, which is why you see every call.
  • It never pretends to be a person.
