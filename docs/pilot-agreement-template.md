# Pilot agreement (DRAFT: have a lawyer read this before any shop signs it)

Between **[Your business name]** ("we") and **[Shop name]** ("you"). Date: **[date]**.

## What this is
When nobody at your shop answers (or the line is busy), your phone forwards the call to a virtual assistant we run.
It answers in your shop's name, books jobs inside your hours, takes messages, and for an emergency writes the
message and then tries your cell. You see every call on your dashboard and in your email, and you get a short
weekly report.

## What it can and cannot do
- It is an AI. It tells callers it is your shop's virtual assistant, and says the call is recorded.
- **It can make mistakes**: mishear a name or a number, book a time you can't keep, or say something you wouldn't.
  Check your dashboard and email each day and tell us about anything wrong the same day; we fix things quickly.
- It does not quote prices for a specific job, give advice about gas, electrical work or opening equipment, or
  diagnose problems. For an emergency it gives the safety instruction (such as "leave the house and call 911").
- It is not a replacement for you being reachable in an emergency. You decide when to turn it on and off.

## You own your facts
Your prices, hours, towns, services, and what you will and won't do are yours. We write them into the assistant only as
you tell us, and we change them only when you tell us. You are responsible for their being right.

## Turning it off
You can stop it in ten seconds, any time, by turning call forwarding off with the code on your checklist. Calls then
ring your own phone again. Tell us too, so we stop the service.

## Your data
- We keep call recordings, transcripts, messages and bookings for **[90]** days, then delete them. Ask us and we delete
  your shop's data sooner.
- Backups of the database are kept for up to 30 days, then expire.
- We use your calls only to run your shop's assistant and to find and fix its mistakes. We do not sell them. Call audio
  and text are processed by our service providers (the phone and voice platform, the AI model provider, email and hosting)
  only to provide the service. **[Lawyer: confirm wording and each provider's terms.]**
- You tell us the caller notice wording you want; it always says the call is recorded and that it is a virtual assistant.
